Data sources. Demographics: U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year estimates via the official Census Data API — ZIP-code and city (place) geographies, updated yearly. Schools: U.S. Dept. of Education Common Core of Data via the Urban Institute Education Data API. Area codes & ZIP-to-state: built-in nationwide reference.

Get your free Census API key (a 1-minute email form): request one at api.census.gov/data/key_signup.html. Then either paste it into the box above (it's saved on your device, so you only do it once) or, to enable it for everyone on your deployed site, paste it into the CENSUS_API_KEY line near the top of this file's script and redeploy. Live demographic and city-list queries require a key; schools work without one.

Privacy note. This tool analyzes aggregate, anonymous neighborhood statistics — never individuals. The Outreach List holds only people who've consented to contact, and stays in your browser unless you export it.
